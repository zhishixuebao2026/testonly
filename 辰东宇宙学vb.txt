Translation:The Three Emperors frowned. This person... was once half-mad. In the Endless Expanse of the Primordial Iands, everything was wiped away by him. From ancient times to the present, nothing remained—only a being like him was left.
Through cycles of destruction and rebirth, repeating endlessly, he reached an indescribable level, an unfathomable height. Then, he grew calm. Looking back, even if he could restore everything in a single thought, revive all his friends and family, he could no longer find the feeling he once had.
Those loved ones, those old friends—their sincere emotions remained unchanged. Yet, to a being like him, they had become like fragments of a bygone era, lines of text, mere traces of information. They lost the meaning they once held for ordinary people.
His state of existence had transcended; he could no longer recover the warmth of the past.
Beyond everything, he is the only one .

Translation:All three stood Above the Sacrificial Path, their cultivation reaching the pinnacle of perfection, truly having walked to the end of the path of cultivation. The realm Above the Sacrificial Path is a state of existence beyond all description, transcending everything. Compared to the Immortal Emperor, it represents an entirely new level of life. Even an Immortal Emperor capable of altering the narrative layer would be considered a mere ordinary being relative to this stratum—a far more eternal and absolute plane of existence, thus qualifying as baseline High 1-A.
